DESCRIPTION

THINK STRAIGHT.

ONO: Think Straight is a minimalist pattern recognition brain defogger puzzle game. A brand new kind of puzzle that relies on your most basic brain functions — motion and prediction.

Below the words of Wordle. Beneath the math of Sudoku.

Start a chain reaction that will leave no O's on the screen (O=NO).

FEATURES

  • Lots of satisfying puzzles for you to solve.
  • 24 levels for you to master from very easy to very challenging.
  • You master a level by solving 4 puzzles, the last in a single try.
  • Levels you have mastered are open for free play.
  • Harder levels have 1000's of unique puzzles for you to try.
  • Rack up points, work on a one-shot streak, or beat your fastest time.

BRAIN WORK

  • Working memory.
  • Pattern recognition.
  • Mental model creation and comparison.
  • Motion prediction.
  • Focus / Attention.
  • Speed.

HOW TO PLAY

You solve a puzzle by choosing the O that will cause a chain reaction that will destroy all the others. Master each level by solving four puzzles — you are allowed three tries in each of the first two, two tries in the next one and, finally, you must solve one puzzle in a single try.

The level is then open for free play with three tries forever. You have an opportunity to watch a :30 ad (or remove ads for .99) to get 2X points on this level to help you run up your score.

Locked levels can be unlocked early by watching a video ad.

Larger levels have 1000's of puzzle variations for endless play.
